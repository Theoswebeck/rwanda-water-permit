Two interaction patterns — each has a different feel for the user.

A — Accordion (one open at a time)

Only one province is expanded at a time. Opening a new one collapses the current. The page stays compact — users compare districts one province at a time.

Pros

  • Page height stays controlled
  • Focused — one province in focus
  • Familiar pattern (FAQ accordions)

Cons

  • Can't compare two provinces' districts side-by-side

B — Multi-expand (any number open at once)

Each province toggles independently. Open as many as you want. Good for users who want to compare districts across two provinces at the same time.

Pros

  • More flexible — compare across provinces
  • User stays in control

Cons

  • Page can get very long if multiple expanded
  • Slightly more complex to scan

My recommendation: A (accordion) — keeps the page compact and most users only need one province at a time. Click to select, then confirm in the terminal.
