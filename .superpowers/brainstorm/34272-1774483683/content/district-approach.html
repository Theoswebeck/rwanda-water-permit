A — Expandable province → district drill-down

Upgrade the existing province card. Click a province to expand its districts inline. One card, two levels of data, no extra scrolling by default.

Pros

  • No new card — saves vertical space
  • Contextual: districts always tied to their province
  • Progressive disclosure — users see what they want

Cons

  • Requires a client interaction pattern (expand/collapse)
  • Districts only visible one province at a time

B — Standalone "Top districts" card (show top 10 + expand)

New card alongside the province card. Shows top districts ranked by permit count, each labelled with its province. "Show all 30" toggle for the full list.

Pros

  • Instantly shows the highest-activity districts
  • Province context on each row
  • Same visual pattern as existing cards — no new component

Cons

  • Adds a 5th card to an already long analytics section
  • No grouping — cross-province comparison harder to see

C — Combined card: province left, top districts right (2-column split)

Merge province and district into one card with a two-column layout. Left = all 5 provinces with bars. Right = top 5 districts ranked, each tagged with province.

Pros

  • Replaces 1 card instead of adding 1 — keeps page length the same
  • Province + district side-by-side = fastest mental model
  • Dense but not cluttered

Cons

  • Two-column inside a card can feel cramped on mobile
  • District bars removed — count-only on right side
