	•	Introduction
AGRA invites qualified consulting firms to submit proposals to provide Consultancy to Develop a Digital Results Management System. This document outlines the requirements and guidelines for the submission of proposals.

	•	Background 
Established in 2006, AGRA is an African-led and Africa-based institution that puts smallholder farmers at the center of the continent’s growing economy by transforming agriculture from a solitary struggle to survive into farming as a business that thrives. Together with our partners, we are working to sustainably grow Africa’s food systems.

In Rwanda, AGRA is focusing on supporting SMEs that are small, informal, and underfinanced, with a special focus on youth and women led SMEs during the current strategy period with interventions that cut through four business lines and three cross-cutting areas. AGRA selected four complementary and integrated business lines: the seed systems, sustainable farming, inclusive markets & trade (IMT), and policy & state capability (PSC). Besides the four business lines, AGRA and its partners selected three cross-cutting areas (a) inclusivity (enabling women and youth to contribute to agriculture for their economic empowerment) (b) climate change adaptation (equipping farmers and strengthening food systems to absorb and recover from shocks, and (c) nutrition (improving nutrition outcomes by diversifying crops and ensuring food availability). The primary business line for Rwanda for the 2023-2027 strategy is inclusive markets and trade (IMT) while being complemented by other business lines to support the country in building inclusive and competitive markets through value addition and trade.

The AGRA Rwanda Country Strategy 2023-2027 focuses on creating employment opportunities for youth and women in the agricultural value chain. More youths are entering Rwanda’s labor market to compete for the limited employment opportunities. Today, 60% of the employed youth are in the agricultural sector. By transforming the agricultural sector, and growing private sector companies, more job, work and entrepreneurship opportunities emerge for young people to gain meaningful (self and wage) employment. As such, AGRA Rwanda is seeking to contribute 132,000 fulfilling and dignifying jobs for youth and women through investment and strengthening of work pathways that benefit them. AGRA is a champion of an inclusive agricultural transformation where youth and women are given the opportunity to play their commensurate role in driving food systems transformation. More information: https://agra.org/

Since January 2023, through its Young Africa Works (YAW) strategy, the MasterCard Foundation Rwanda launched a series of strategic partnerships with a selection of nine (9) organizations aligned with the implementation of the Agri-Food System Portfolio. Key partners include, CARE Rwanda, Trade Mark Africa, Access to Finance Rwanda, BPN, ESPartners, WFP, BRD and SNV. Together, the partnerships form a unified approach which aims to catalyze achievement of dignified and fulfilling work for young people, especially young women, and disadvantaged groups, through the growth of inclusive food systems and agriculture value chains. The collaboration of the partners aims to achieve the following:

	•	Advance education by providing training, mentorship, and work experience to financially disadvantaged young people in Rwanda to develop their skills needed for work and entrepreneurship in the agricultural sector; and
	•	Relieve poverty by creating employment and entrepreneurship opportunities for financially disadvantaged individuals, together with economic development in disadvantaged communities, through targeted interventions in the agricultural sector in Rwanda.

The initiative focuses on young women and men aged between 15 and 35 who are not in employment, education or training, as aligned with the Foundation’s “Young Africa Works” strategy of enabling 30 million Young Africans to access dignified and fulfilling work by 2030 (with young women making up 70 percent of this target).

Through the above partnership, AGRA is implementing a 5-year program, the Youth Engagement for the Future of Food and Agriculture (YEFFA). The AGRA Rwanda country strategy recognizes that the success of young entrepreneurs in agriculture hinges on a well-coordinated ecosystem that provides the support they need. However, this ecosystem often experiences weaknesses, including broken links that can hinder youth entrepreneurship in agriculture. 

Under the Mastercard Foundation Agri-Food System Portfolio, AGRA works as the Super Aggregator to facilitate the coordination of the various partners involved to develop an acceptable mechanism to regularly discuss portfolio results. Recognizing the diversity of partnerships under Agri-Food System Portfolio and to achieve a coordinated ecosystem that provides youth the support they need.

Specifically, AGRA will provide the following services:- 
	•	Facilitate the coordination of partners and establish mechanisms for regular Agri-Food System Portfolio result discussions and convenings to include.
	•	Lead periodic analysis of Agri-Food System Portfolio outcomes and develop knowledge products such as policy briefs, impact stories, and white papers. 
	•	Establish a shared knowledge learning platform for showcasing progress and addressing challenges of Agri-Food System Portfolio. 
	•	Provide technical leadership and facilitate discussions to achieve a shared understanding of Agri-Food System Portfolio results and impacts. 
	•	Identify and provide strategic technical assistance to inform key Agri-Food System Portfolio outcomes. 
	•	Establish and maintain active Communities of Practice (CoP).

2. Objectives 
The overall objective of the assignment is to develop a Digital Results Management System (DRMS) to support ongoing collaboration and coordination among consortium partners by providing aggregation and analysis of results at the portfolio level. The DRMS will build on an approved joint results framework which is a synthesis of each partner's results frameworks and related monitoring, evaluation, and learning (MEL) plan. The DRMS will include a high-level dashboard that is distilled from the overall results to be periodically updated online through individual entries of key results achieved by each of the partners. Currently, the members of the consortium have their respective institutional results measurement systems and platforms for reporting and measuring the progress and results of interventions.

The development of a digital results management system will build on a robust data management system as one of the core components to ensure the management of results and evidence-based programming contributing towards the core principles of results-based management (RBM).

To this effect, AGRA seeks to hire an experienced consultancy firm to develop the Digital Results Management System, that includes an integrated overall results measurement system and an automated dashboard for Mastercard Foundation Agri-Food System portfolio partners to report on and visualize the progress and results. The DRMS will have the following benefits:
	•	Provide an easily accessible platform for data entry, analysis and visualization from results of portfolio implementation.
	•	Serve as a shared platform for aligning reporting and assessing portfolio performance results among Agri-Food System Portfolio partners for decision making and continual portfolio improvement.
	•	Provide a summary results dashboard to facilitate the partners involved to develop a mechanism to regularly discuss portfolio results.

	•	Scope of Work 
Specifically, the development process of the Digital Results Management System (DRMS) will begin with an assessment of the existing partners’ data management systems to establish the required needs for an integrated management system that would draw from the existing systems of reporting to the extent possible. This will consider the key indicators to be determined by the Super Aggregator against which program results are measured. The successful firm will liaise with AGRA to review the Joint results framework developed to ensure it articulates the Foundation’s key country commitments under the YAW program in Rwanda and to which all the partners are contributing. The DRMS should align with each partner’s key interventions and outcomes, as well as the overall MEL plan, which outlines key indicators and periodic targets of each portfolio partner. Additionally, the system should have the capability to identify and eliminate duplicates when necessary.

The firm will also develop an automated dashboard for periodic reporting and results visualization. Agreed upon key metrics will be uploaded into the central web-based dashboard accessed by all partners and the Foundation, with necessary in-built approval levels. The dashboard will be a key component of review in the all-partner periodic review meetings, as a basis of gauging progress towards commitments, generating lessons, and continual program improvement. Based on the Joint Results Framework, the agreed upon KPIs, Portfolio Partners MEL plan, the high-level dashboard will be established.

	•	Methodology 

The consultancy will adopt a participatory and phased approach, starting with a detailed assessment of existing data management systems and stakeholder needs. This will inform the definition of technical and functional requirements, ensuring alignment with the Joint Results Framework and partner MEL plans. The system will be designed in phases, incorporating stakeholder feedback through prototypes and testing phases. Integration with existing partner platforms and duplicate data management will be prioritized. Capacity building and documentation will accompany development to ensure user readiness. The pilot phase will validate system performance and usability, with lessons incorporated before full rollout.
